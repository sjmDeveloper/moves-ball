<h1>How to Do This</h1>
<h2>Green circle:</h2>
<h3>$$(S): \frac{X^2}{a^2} + \frac{Y^2}{b^2} = 1, \quad \text{set } x=\frac{X}{a}, \quad y=\frac{Y}{b}$$</h3>
<h3>$$\forall z \in \mathbb{C}, \forall x \in \mathbb{R}, \forall y \in \mathbb{R}, \exists t \in \mathbb{R}, \quad |z| = 10 \implies z=e^{it}, \quad z=x+iy \qquad z=\cos(t) + i\sin(t) \iff x=\sin(t) \quad \text{and} \quad y=\cos(t)$$</h3>
<h3>$$Y/a = \sin(t), \quad Y=a \cdot \sin(t), \quad \text{and} \quad X/b= \cos(t), \quad X=b \cdot \cos(t)$$</h3>
<h2>Yellow circle:</h2>
<h3>I used sqrt on the X-axis because it gives a slow movement.</h3>
<h3>I used tan on the Y-axis because $\lim_{x \to \frac{\pi}{2}} \tan(x) = +\infty$, which places the ball at the bottom.</h3>
